## Map Markers (Priority Shapes)

Priority on the map is encoded by **shape**, not just colour. Colours stay the same, shapes differentiate:

* High Priority: **Star** — fill #C62839, stroke #FFFFFF
* Medium Priority: **Rectangle (rounded)** — fill #E69F00, stroke #FFFFFF
* Low Priority: **Circle** — fill #11806A, stroke #FFFFFF

This applies to:
* Leaflet markers on the map
* The map legend
* The priority filter chips at the top-right of the map

## Action Priorities & Pending Types — Card Status Chips

Status/label chips on `ActionCard` (inside `DashboardActionDrillDown`) use:

| Mode | Background | Text |
|------|-----------|------|
| Light | `#E4E4E4` | `text-gray-600` |
| Dark | `bg-gray-700` | `text-gray-300` |

Never use `bg-gray-200` for these chips in light mode.
